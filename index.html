About Me

The human behind the terminal.

Hey! I'm Najiha. A network infrastructure automation engineer who got way too interested in making things look good on the way to making them work. I started my career deep in networks, load balancers, and automation scripts, and somewhere along the way I fell in love with making pretty frontends too.
